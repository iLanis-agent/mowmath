Mow math

A mow goes wrong in the driveway: the tank runs dry with two stripes to go, the last pass is a mower-width sliver, or the bag fills before the first turn. Mow math turns the lawn, the deck and your pace into honest minutes, turns the lawn width into the pass count and the last stripe's width, and turns the clippings into the bag fills. Exact arithmetic, every borrowed number labeled.

Open the calculators

The three tools

  • Time plan - the lawn, the deck at labeled overlap and your pace become the minutes.
  • Stripe plan - the lawn width at the deck width becomes the passes and the last stripe.
  • Bag plan - the lawn at the labeled clipping rate becomes the litres and the bag fills.

Labeled norms

Labeled norms: 85% of the deck width after overlap, 1.2 L of clippings per m2, and a 40 L catcher bag - all illustrative defaults you can change by measuring. Slopes, wet grass and dull blades change everything; the mower's manual governs the real mow. This is time and layout arithmetic, not a promise about your Saturday.
